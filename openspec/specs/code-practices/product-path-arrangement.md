# Test arrangement: reach state through the product path

An arrange or act step reaches a namespace the way the product reaches it. When a scenario needs a pod's stores on some node, the pods service and the armer are what put them there — invite and join, or link a device and let the armer open the pod from its identity's PMS, and poll for convergence. A hand-made handover (mint a pod's tickets on one node, `import_pod` them on another) is a shortcut to a state the product would have produced, and a test arranged that way keeps passing after the mechanism it stands in for breaks: the armer can stop opening pods entirely and the assertions still see the data the tickets delivered.

A hand-made handover is admissible in exactly two places. First, where the import is itself the subject — the data layer's `import_pod` has its own scenarios, and they rest on a bare ticket on purpose. Second, where it is the negative control the [access-control-tests](access-control-tests.md) practice requires — the holder of a pod's tickets that is no member, whose tickets must address the very device under test and which no product path hands them to. Anywhere else, arrange through the product path.

An admitted instrument is named, never silent: the test's docs say what the hand-made step is for and why the product path cannot supply it (the ticket-holder control of the worked example below — it needs tickets addressing the owner's device, and no product path hands a pod's tickets to a non-member). Observation-only helpers behind the `test-util` feature (forced writes, session watches, contact-set reads) are instruments of the same kind: admitted, annotated, absent from product builds.

Every rewrite from a hand-made arrangement onto the product path is checked against the deliberately broken mechanism before it is accepted — the armer disabled, the sweep reverted — and must fail there. That check is the only thing that distinguishes a rewrite from a rearrangement: if the assertions still pass with the mechanism removed, they were never testing it.

The worked example of the whole rule is `devices_linked_before_and_after_the_join_reach_the_pod` in `pdn-node/tests/pods.rs`: the pod, the join and the linked devices all cross by the pods service, the linking dialogue and the armer's sweep; no import act appears in the arrangement; the serving device is isolated by taking the member's other devices offline rather than by aiming a ticket at it; and the ticket-holder control beside it is the one admitted handover.
