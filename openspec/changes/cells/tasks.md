# Tasks: cells

Scope: the cell as a platform primitive — a keyless id, two stores per cell — the membership store and the record store — replicated whole among member devices, the membership store first, every entry a served session carries held and judged by the membership fold and the record view, membership with owners joined under a one-time secret, records — claims, mergeable-documents and immutable-documents — as content. Nothing existing changes shape.
