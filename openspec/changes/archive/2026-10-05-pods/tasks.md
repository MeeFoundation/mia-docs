# Tasks: pods

Scope: the pod as a platform primitive — a keyless id, two stores per pod — the membership store and the record store — replicated whole among member devices, the membership store first, every entry a served session carries held and judged by the membership fold and the record view, membership with owners joined under a one-time secret, records — claims, mergeable-documents and immutable-documents — as content. Nothing existing changes shape.

## 7. Stress

- [x] 7.1 The full stress pass on join, kick and relay scenarios, the in-process join and sync of two co-located members included (`--stress-count`, per the flaky-tests practice), the one after 4.4 having been cut at 50 iterations; the failure that pass left undiagnosed diagnosed in isolation first: `a_write_arrives_live_over_the_swarm_and_a_ticket_holder_takes_nothing` in data-layer's `tests/pod_delivery.rs`, a claim not reaching a member's device over the swarm within the wait, the two membership writes before it delivered — once in 50 iterations of the loaded pool, after the change watch began deriving at once on an arriving entry or payload, and never in 86 more with the engine traced.
